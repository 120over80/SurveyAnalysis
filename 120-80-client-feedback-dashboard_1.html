Client feedback: what actually drives a high score

120/80 GROUP relationship assessment · 2025 and 2026 · 59 responses across 35 client accounts

The short answer

Which qualities correlate with a high score. Four findings, both years agree.

  1. Strategy is the swing factor. In 2026, clients who called strategic thinking a strength averaged 9.1. Clients who asked for better strategy averaged 7.2. That 1.9-point spread is the widest of any quality, and it repeats in 2025.
  2. Proactivity is the weakest link and it matters most. Of the three qualities we ask clients to rate, proactivity scores lowest in both years (7.8 in 2025, 7.9 in 2026) and tracks the recommend score most closely. Understanding of their business and our tools both score higher and move the needle less.
  3. Client service is table stakes, not a differentiator. Around 90% of clients name it a strength no matter what they score us. Almost nobody asks us to improve it. It keeps clients, it does not lift scores.
  4. Low raters and high raters ask for different things. Low raters want strategy and results. High raters, when they ask for anything, want measurement and sharper writing. Same firm, two different conversations.

Section 6

What to do about it

Five moves the data points to. Each one names the number behind it.

1. Put a strategy moment on every retainer

Strategy is the widest score swing we have: +1.9 for clients who call it a strength, −1.9 for clients who want more of it. Nobody in the high group asked for better strategy. A standing quarterly session where we bring the idea rather than take the brief is the cheapest way to move a passive into a promoter. Three clients said a version of this unprompted: Machinify wrote "we're pulling work out of you rather than having you drive us," Infinitus wrote "most of the strategy is coming from our team," and epocrates wrote they are not getting "the same proactive coverage and ideation."

2. Treat proactivity as the number we manage

It is our lowest rated quality two years running, 7.8 then 7.9, and it tracks the recommend score more closely than understanding their business or our tools do. Everything else improved in 2026. This one did not. If one metric goes on a monthly dashboard, make it this.

3. Stop selling client service and start selling results

93% of clients name client service a strength and almost nobody asks us to improve it, so it no longer differentiates us. Results is the opposite: 39% want it stronger, and it is the second widest score swing. Renewal conversations should lead with the coverage and pipeline story, not responsiveness.

4. Give measurement to the clients who are already happy

Measurement is the one thing high raters ask for more than low raters do: 42% of the high group flagged it against 25% of the low group. These are the clients defending our budget internally. COTA spelled it out: they need to measure impact "in an evidence based way" to make the case for expanding services. Better reporting here protects revenue rather than repairing it.

5. Work the four low accounts individually, not as a theme

The 2026 low group is only four responses, and their reasons differ. Infinitus and epocrates want strategic ownership. Optura wants results and clearer scope. Lantern wants proactive ideas and analytics. Averaging them hides the fix. Each needs its own conversation.

Worth a second look

MKTG posts a 92 NPS in 2026 against AOX3 at 50, but MKTG had 12 responses to AOX3's 14 and a different client mix, and the two agencies were much closer in 2025 (56 against 77, the other way round). With numbers this small, one unhappy account moves the score 15 points. Treat the gap as a prompt to look, not as a finding.

The honest caveat. 28 responses in 2026 and 31 in 2025 is a small base, and the low group is four people each year. Everything here is directional. The patterns are worth acting on because both years point the same way, not because any single percentage is precise. Raising the response rate above 45% would do more for next year's analysis than any new question.
